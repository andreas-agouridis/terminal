# Getting Started: Coffee via SSH

Terminal's SSH interface provides a convenient way to order coffee directly from your command line. This guide will walk you through the process from connection to checkout.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, you'll need:

- An SSH client installed on your system
- A Terminal account
- Your SSH key added to your Terminal account

:::tip[New to SSH?]
Check out our [SSH Overview](/docs/ssh-overview) if you're new to SSH or need a refresher.
:::

## Connecting to the Terminal Coffee Shop

Connect to our SSH server using:

```bash
ssh terminal.com
```

## Navigating the Coffee Menu

Once connected, you'll see the Terminal UI interface. Here's how to navigate:

1. Use arrow keys (`↑` `↓`) to move between menu options
2. Press `Enter` to select an option
3. Press `Esc` to go back to the previous menu

### Main Menu Options

- **Shop**: Browse coffee products
- **Cart**: View your current selections
- **Account**: Manage your account settings
- **Orders**: View order history
- **Help**: Access documentation

## Managing Your Cart

To view and modify your cart:

1. Navigate to **Cart** from the main menu
2. Use arrow keys to select items
3. Press `+` to increase quantity
4. Press `-` to decrease quantity
5. Press `x` to remove an item
6. Press `c` to continue to checkout

## Adding Payment Methods

You can add payment methods through either SSH or HTTPS:

### Via SSH

1. Navigate to **Account** in the main menu
2. Select **Payment Methods**
3. Choose **Add New Card**
4. Enter your card details when prompted

:::caution[Secure Input]
Your payment information is encrypted end-to-end and never stored in plain text.
:::

### Via HTTPS

You can also manage payment methods through our web interface:

1. Visit [https://terminal.com/account/payments](https://terminal.com/account/payments)
2. Log in to your account
3. Click **Add Payment Method**
4. Fill in the form and submit

:::tip[Multiple Payment Methods]
Add multiple payment methods to quickly switch between them at checkout.
:::

## Order Tracking

After placing an order, you can track its status:

1. Select **Orders** from the main menu
2. Choose the order you wish to track
3. View real-time status updates

Order status will change from:
- **Processing** → **Brewing** → **Out for Delivery** → **Delivered**

## Tips and Tricks

- **Quick Navigation**: Press `h` to return home, `q` to quit
- **Search**: Press `/` followed by search terms to find products
- **Favorites**: Press `f` when viewing a product to add it to favorites
- **Reorder**: In the orders menu, press `r` on a past order to quickly reorder

## Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues during the ordering process:

- Check your SSH connection with `ssh -v terminal.com`
- Ensure your payment method is valid
- Verify your delivery address is complete
- Contact support via `support@terminal.com` or within the app by pressing `?`

:::note[SSH Session Timeout]
Your SSH session will timeout after 30 minutes of inactivity. Any unsaved cart items will be preserved for your next session.
:::
